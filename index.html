Scientists Pair Machine Learning With Quantum Physics to Discover Two New Superconductors
Researchers have combined machine learning with quantum physics calculations to identify two previously unknown superconductors, YRu3B2 and LuRu3B2, which get their superconductivity from electrons forming "flat bands" in a hexagonal kagome lattice pattern.
