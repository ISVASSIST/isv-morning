COP31 Climate Summit Collapses With No Final Deal
The COP31 climate talks in Antalya, Turkey, ended on Sunday without a final agreement. The summit's president told delegates the proposed "Antalya Pact" had failed. The main fight was over fossil fuels. The final draft left out any direct mention of phasing out coal, oil and gas, and the EU, the UK and small island nations wouldn't accept it. Climate finance was the other dealbreaker, according to Australia's Chris Bowen, who helped lead the negotiations. The talks ran more than 44 hours past their scheduled Friday finish, the longest ever. A few smaller decisions survived, and the 2023 Dubai agreement to transition away from fossil fuels still stands.

🔭 SPACE
Webb Telescope Finds a Black Hole That Grew Faster Than Its Own Galaxy
Astronomers using the James Webb Space Telescope have found what may be an "overmassive" black hole in a young galaxy called MARTA-3887, seen as it was in the early universe. The black hole's mass is about 2.3% of the mass of all the galaxy's stars, which is roughly 100 times more than we'd expect from galaxies nearby. That suggests the black hole grew faster than the galaxy built its stars. The team, led by Mirko Curti of Italy's INAF, used several lines of evidence. The work is still a preprint and hasn't been peer reviewed yet. Phys.org covered it on 22 November.
💡 Business Insight
When an AI Rings Your Business for a Quote, Make Sure It Hears the Right Answer
AI assistants like Gemini are starting to phone businesses on customers' behalf to check hours, availability and prices. That means the first call about a job might come from a computer, not a person. Before that happens, decide how you want to handle it. Write a short script for whoever answers the phone: what services you offer, your usual lead time, whether you do site visits, and that quotes are only given after you've seen the job. Then make sure your Google Business Profile matches the script, with correct hours, services, service area and a phone number that's actually answered. If you'd rather not deal with robot callers, you can switch them off in the profile's Advanced settings. Either way, never give a firm price over the phone to an AI. Offer to send a written quote instead, so you keep control of the paperwork.
