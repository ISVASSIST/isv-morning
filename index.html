💡 Business Insight
Give AI the Least Access It Needs: Keep Customer and Bank Details Out of Chat Tools
Microsoft is adding controls this week to limit rogue AI agents, a sign that even big vendors worry about tools with too much access. Small trades can apply the same idea for free. Paste in job descriptions, quote wording and site notes, but leave out customer phone numbers, bank details and logins. Use a separate login for AI tools, avoid giving any assistant direct access to your accounting or banking, and review anything it drafts before it goes out. Start with drafting and summarising, where a mistake is easy to catch.
