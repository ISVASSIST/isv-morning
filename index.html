💡 Business Insight
Let AI Draft a One-Page Site Induction Sheet for New Workers and Subbies
Every new casual or subbie needs the same briefing: site rules, PPE, emergency contacts, who to call, where the gear lives, and what not to touch. Give an AI tool your existing notes and SWMS and ask it to turn them into a single plain-English page, with a short checklist at the bottom to sign. Check every safety detail yourself before it's used, because the AI can invent things that sound right. Print it, laminate it, and hand it over on day one.
