💡 Business Insight
Write Your Quoting Process Down First: AI Can Only Copy a Process You Can Describe
New AI tools keep getting cheaper and faster, but they do the same job you give them, which is only as clear as your instructions. Before you pay for another tool, spend an hour writing down how you quote: what you measure, which prices you check, what you add for travel and fuel, and what you always forget. That one page becomes the instruction sheet for any AI helper and a checklist for staff, and it exposes the guesswork that quietly eats margin. Start with your most common job type and test the page on a past quote.
