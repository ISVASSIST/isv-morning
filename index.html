The government's national gas reservation scheme was set to pass the Senate late on Thursday after the Coalition said it wouldn't oppose the bill, the ABC reported. 🔬 Science
🌌 ASTRONOMY · PULSARS
Runaway Pulsar Caught Escaping the Wreckage of Its Own Supernova at 1,400 km/s
Astronomers led by Rohit Dokara have found a pulsar, J1909+0749, that has raced right out of the shell of debris left by the supernova that created it. A pulsar is the spinning, collapsed core of a dead star. This one is crossing the sky at more than 1,400 km per second, one of the fastest ever measured in the Milky Way. The team spotted the supernova remnant, G42.1-0.2, in radio images from South Africa's MeerKAT telescope, and found the pulsar sitting outside it by chance while doing a routine analysis of radio surveys. The results are published in Astronomy & Astrophysics. Only four cases are known where a pulsar has outrun its own supernova shell. Pulsars like this help scientists work out how lopsided supernova explosions kick dead stars across the galaxy.
