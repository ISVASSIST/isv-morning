💡 Business Insight
Let AI Draft Your Complaint and Defect Replies: You Check the Facts, Then Press Send
A tense email from a customer is hard to answer at 6pm after a long day. Paste the complaint into an AI tool, add your job notes and photos, and ask for a calm, factual draft that offers a fix and a date. Then read every line: check the dates, the spec and any promise it makes, because the tool can invent details. You stay in charge of what goes out, but the drafting that used to take 30 minutes takes five, and your tone stays professional when you're tired.
