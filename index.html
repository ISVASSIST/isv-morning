💡 Business Insight
When Outlook and Teams Go Down, Have AI Write Your One-Page Outage Plan Now
Friday's global Microsoft outage was a reminder that your email, calendar and job notes can vanish for hours without warning. Ask an AI tool to draft a short outage plan: which customers to phone first, where a printed copy of this week's jobs and contacts lives, which personal phone or text number to use instead, and who tells the crew. Keep it on the fridge in the workshop and in the ute. It takes ten minutes, and it means the next outage costs you a coffee break instead of a day's work.
