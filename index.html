💡 Business Insight
Let AI Chase Your Overdue Invoices Politely
Cashflow is where small trades businesses get squeezed, especially with fuel and rates up. Paste your overdue invoice list, with names, amounts and days overdue, into an AI tool and ask for three reminder emails: friendly, firm and final. Check each one for tone and the figures before sending, and keep customer details out of the tool if you can. Fifteen minutes a week on this tends to pull payments forward without a single awkward phone call.
