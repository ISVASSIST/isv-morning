💡 Business Insight
Sandbox First: Trial Any AI Agent on Dummy Jobs Before It Touches Real Customers
Anthropic has just locked its own test agents away from the live internet because it cannot yet fully control them. If the company that built the tool does that, a small trades business should too. Before you let an AI assistant send quotes, chase invoices or answer customers, run it on a few made-up jobs and a spare email address. Read what it would have sent, fix the instructions, and only then switch it on for real. An hour of dummy runs is far cheaper than one embarrassing email to a good client.
