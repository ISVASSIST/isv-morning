💡 Business Insight
Photograph Every Job Before and After: Your Best Training Data for Any AI Tool
Most AI tools get useful only when they have your own examples to learn from. Start a simple habit now: take a dated before and after photo of every job, name it with the client, the surface and the spec, and keep it in one folder. In six months you will have a library that can illustrate quotes, settle disputes about condition, train new staff and feed any tool that estimates from photos. It costs nothing, takes two minutes a job, and makes you ready when the tools improve.
