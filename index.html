💡 Business Insight
Ask AI to Turn Messy Supplier Emails Into a One-Page Order Summary
Quantities, delivery dates and price changes get buried in long email chains, and that is where wrong orders and surprise freight charges come from. Paste the thread into an AI tool and ask for a one-page table of items, quantities, prices, delivery dates and anything still unconfirmed. Check it against the original quote before you place the order, and leave out account numbers and card details. It takes a couple of minutes and catches the small mistakes that eat margin.
