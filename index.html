💡 Business Insight
Let AI Turn Your Rough Site Notes Into a Clean Variation Request
Extra work found mid-job is where small trades quietly lose money, usually because the change was agreed verbally and never written down. After the conversation, type or voice-record rough notes on what changed, why, and the extra time and materials, then ask an AI tool to turn them into a short, polite variation request with a clear price line for the customer to approve. Check the figures and wording yourself before sending, and keep customer personal details out of the tool. It takes two minutes and protects your margin.
