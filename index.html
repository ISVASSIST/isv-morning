❤️ HEALTH SCIENCE
Energy Drinks and Stimulant Supplements Linked to 11 Times More Cardiac Arrest Reports
A preliminary study presented at the American Heart Association's Scientific Sessions in Chicago reviewed more than 28,000 US FDA adverse-event reports from 2017 to 2025. Reports involving caffeinated energy drinks or stimulant supplements were about 11 times more likely to mention cardiac arrest and 4.5 times more likely to mention a heart attack. About 45% involved people aged 18 to 34. It shows an association, not proof of cause.
💡 Business Insight
Use AI to Write a One-Page AI Rules Sheet for Your Crew
With Canberra's draft guide on AI at work out this week, it's a good time to set a few simple rules in your own business. Ask an AI tool to draft a one-page sheet covering what staff can use it for (quotes, emails, SWMS drafts), what they must never paste in (client details, pricing, bank info), and that a person always checks anything before it goes to a customer. Read it, cut what doesn't fit, and go through it at the next toolbox talk. Ten minutes now beats cleaning up a privacy mistake later.
