💡 Business Insight
The White House 'Self-Policing' AI Pledge — Why Your Business Still Needs Its Own AI Rules
Big AI companies just signed a voluntary accord to police themselves, but it creates no enforceable rules, so protecting your business is still on you. Write a one-page policy: which tools staff may use, what customer and pricing data must never go into them, who checks AI-drafted quotes and emails before they're sent, and who to tell if something goes wrong. Ask every AI vendor where your data is stored and whether it trains their models. A simple policy costs an afternoon and is far cheaper than explaining a leaked quote to a client.
