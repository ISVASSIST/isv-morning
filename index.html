💡 Business Insight
Let AI Write Plain-English Aftercare Sheets: Cure Times and Care Instructions Customers Will Actually Read
After a coating or repair job, most customer callbacks come down to something simple: how long before it can get wet, what not to use, when to recoat. Give AI the product data sheet and ask it for a one-page plain-English handover with cure times, do's and don'ts, and who to call. Put your own details at the top and keep your standard version as a template. Check every time figure against the manufacturer's data sheet before it goes out, because AI can get numbers wrong. A clear sheet cuts phone calls and protects you if there's a dispute later.
