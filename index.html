🔬 Science
🧠 HEALTH
Flashing Light-and-Sound Headset Shows No Benefit for Alzheimer's in a Large Trial
A year-long trial of Cognito Therapeutics' Spectris headset, which uses flickering lights and sounds to stimulate 40 Hz "gamma" brain waves, found no cognitive benefit for people with Alzheimer's, Nature reports. The 673 participants used either the real device or a sham for an hour a day. After 12 months, the group using the real device did no better than the sham group. The results were presented at the Clinical Trials on Alzheimer's Disease meeting in Boston on 16 November. One investigator said it seemed to work better in milder cases, but that will need another trial to prove.
