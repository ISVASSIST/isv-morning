🌌 JAMES WEBB · EXOPLANET
Webb Telescope Finds Exoplanet 700 Light-Years Away With a Predictable Daily Weather Cycle — Mineral Clouds Every Morning, Gone by Nightfall
NASA's James Webb Space Telescope has identified a gas giant approximately 700 light-years from Earth that experiences a strikingly regular daily weather pattern — mineral clouds composed of silicate particles (essentially suspended grains of sand) form in the planet's upper atmosphere each morning, then burn off by nightfall as atmospheric temperature rises. The finding was made using Webb's spectrographic instruments tracking atmospheric composition and timing across multiple orbital observations of the tidally locked world. It is the first time a regularly repeating weather cycle has been confirmed on an exoplanet, and it demonstrates that weather modelling — not just atmospheric detection — is now scientifically feasible for worlds hundreds of light-years away. The clouds are made of the same minerals found in industrial ceramics and coatings here on Earth.

🧠 Things Worth Knowing
◆The honeybee communicates the exact direction and distance of a food source to its hive mates through a behaviour called the waggle dance — the angle of the dance relative to vertical indicates the direction relative to the sun, and the duration of the waggle run encodes the distance, with roughly one second corresponding to one kilometre. Austrian ethologist Karl von Frisch spent decades decoding this system and was awarded the Nobel Prize in Physiology or Medicine in 1973 for it — one of the rare Nobel Prizes awarded for deciphering animal communication.
◆The Great Pacific Garbage Patch — floating between Hawaii and California — is roughly three times the size of France at approximately 1.6 million square kilometres. But 94% of the plastic items in it are microplastics invisible to the naked eye, not the floating island of waste that popular imagery suggests. The Ocean Cleanup project has removed more than 9 million kilograms from it since 2018 using passive autonomous systems that drift with ocean currents and concentrate debris without active propulsion.
◆Duct tape was invented in 1943 after a factory worker named Vesta Stoudt wrote directly to President Roosevelt arguing that the wax-sealed ammunition boxes her sons were using overseas couldn't be opened quickly enough in the field. Roosevelt forwarded the letter to the War Production Board, and Revolite Corporation developed the tape — originally called duck tape after the cotton duck fabric used — which became standard US military issue within months. 3M commercialised it for civilian HVAC use after the war, and the name evolved from duck to duct.
