🔬 Science
🔋 BATTERIES
Scientists Build a Rechargeable Battery That Uses Vitamin B12
Researchers at the University of Córdoba in Spain have built a rechargeable zinc-air battery that uses vitamin B12 as a catalyst. The vitamin isn't the fuel. It helps drive the chemical reactions when the battery charges and discharges, thanks to the cobalt atom at its centre. Because B12 dissolves in water, the team attached it to tiny carbon nanotubes to keep it in place. The prototype lasted up to 250 charge cycles. It's still early lab work, but it shows a cheap, natural molecule could one day replace expensive metals in batteries. ScienceDaily covered the study on 20 November.
💡 Business Insight
After a Storm, Let AI Turn Your Damage Photos Into a Claim-Ready Report
If today's storms do any damage to the shed, the yard or a job in progress, how quickly you document it can decide how smoothly your insurance claim goes. Before you clean anything up, walk around and take photos and short videos of every bit of damage, with something in the shot for scale. Then upload them to ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini and ask it to write a damage report: a list of each item, where it is, what's wrong with it, and the date and time it happened. Add the BoM warning for the day and ask it to draft a short, factual email to your insurer or broker. Check every detail yourself before you send it, and don't let the AI guess at costs or causes. Keep the original photos in a folder you own.
