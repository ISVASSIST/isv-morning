💡 Business Insight
Ask AI to Compare Supplier Price Lists and Flag Increases Before You Quote
With fuel and freight costs moving, supplier prices drift up in small steps that are easy to miss. Paste last month's and this month's price list into an AI tool and ask it to list every line that went up, by how much, and which jobs in your pipeline use those items. Use the result to update quotes before they go out. Check the numbers yourself against the original invoices, and leave customer names and bank details out of what you paste. It takes about ten minutes and can save you from quoting on yesterday's prices.
