💡 Business Insight
Let AI Draft a Scope-Confirmation Message Before You Start Any New Job
Most disputes start with a vague quote. Paste your quote and site notes into an AI tool and ask it to write a short, plain-English message listing what's included, what's excluded, access needs, and what happens if conditions differ. Send it to the customer before the first day and ask for a reply to confirm. Read it carefully first, because the AI can add things you never agreed to. Five minutes up front can save an argument over a variation later.
