⚡ ENERGY
America's Biggest Data Centres Now Use as Much Power as Some US States
A Harvard-led study in Nature Sustainability mapped 403 hyperscale data centres across the United States, the giant facilities that run AI and cloud services. It estimates they used 68 to 99 terawatt-hours of electricity a year, roughly as much as some US states, and about 54% of it came from fossil fuels. The authors say the pollution from that generation falls on communities near power stations, although they did not directly measure health effects. With AI use still growing, the study shows where its energy actually comes from.
💡 Business Insight
Let AI Check Your Bank Statements for Fees You Never Agreed To
ASIC is suing CommBank, alleging that about 42,000 business customers were charged CommBiz fees they were never properly told about, many of them 25 cents at a time. Small fees like that are easy to miss in a busy month. Export the last three months of business bank and merchant statements as CSV and remove the account numbers. Then ask ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini to list every fee by type and how often it appears, and to flag anything new, duplicated or changed. Compare that list with your bank's current fee schedule. If something doesn't match, raise it with the bank in writing. Even a few dollars a week adds up over a year.
