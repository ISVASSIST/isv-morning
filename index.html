🧠 NEUROSCIENCE · USA
Scientists Complete the First Full Brain Map of Any Adult Animal — 160,000 Neurons and 50 Million Connections in a Fruit Fly
An international team led by Harvard Medical School and Princeton University has published the first complete connectome of an adult animal's entire nervous system — mapping every neuron and synapse in a fruit fly's brain and nerve cord at electron microscope resolution. The Brain and Nerve Cord (BANC) dataset captures all ~160,000 neurons and over 50 million connections, assembled from thousands of microscopy images stitched together by AI. The key finding: most fly behaviours appear to be directed by local neural circuits distributed through the body, not by a single central command in the brain. The full dataset is freely available online as an open-source resource for neuroscience globally. Published in Nature, June 2026.
