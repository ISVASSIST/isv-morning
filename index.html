💡 Business Insight
Ask AI to Turn Your Fuel Receipts Into a Cost-Per-Job Report
With diesel still a major cost, most small trades only see fuel as one lump on the credit card. Photograph or export a month of fuel receipts, add your job list and rough distances, and ask an AI tool to estimate fuel cost per job and flag the runs that eat your margin. Check its arithmetic against a couple of jobs you know, and keep customer names out of it. If a regular far-flung job is losing money, that's a price conversation, not a guess.
