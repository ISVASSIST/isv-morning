💡 Business Insight
Ask AI to Turn Supplier Price Rises Into a Quote Update Checklist
With fuel and freight costs moving, quotes go stale fast. Paste your last few supplier invoices and an old quote into an AI tool and ask it to list every line where the cost has changed, the percentage difference, and a suggested new price with a short note for the customer. Check the numbers against the real invoices, because it can misread figures. Ten minutes of this protects your margin on jobs you haven't started yet.
