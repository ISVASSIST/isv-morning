💊 MEDICINE
Stopping Statins After 70 Looks Safe in the Short Term, but Heart Risk Creeps Up Later
The STREAM trial followed about 1,900 adults aged 70 and over who took statins only for prevention, with no previous heart attack or stroke. It was published in the New England Journal of Medicine. At two years, stopping was no worse than continuing for heart attacks, strokes or death, and quality of life, muscle pain and falls didn't differ. By four years, though, heart events were a little higher in the group that stopped. The lead author says it doesn't support a general rule to stop. Talk to your GP before changing any medication.
💡 Business Insight
Before You Paste Anything Into an AI Tool, Strip Out the Passwords and Bank Details
The Notion breach is a reminder that anything you type into an AI tool can pass through other companies' servers. Make one simple rule for the business: no passwords, bank details, tax file numbers or customer home addresses go into ChatGPT, Claude, Copilot or any other AI tool. Swap them for placeholders like [CUSTOMER] or [SITE] before you paste a quote, email or invoice in. You can even ask the AI to remind you to remove sensitive details first. It takes ten seconds a job, and it means a breach at someone else's company doesn't become your problem.
